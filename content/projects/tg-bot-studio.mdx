# TG Bot Studio

A self-hosted web application for managing multiple Telegram bots and
Telegram Mini Apps (TMAs) from a single dashboard.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/sametcn99/tg-bot-studio)

## What is it?

Running more than one Telegram bot usually means a pile of scripts, config
files, and long-running processes that you babysit by hand. TG Bot Studio
replaces that with one web dashboard you host yourself: add your bot tokens,
and the studio keeps the bots online, stores their settings in its own
database, and gives you a browser interface for everything the bots do.

## What can it do?

- **Build conversations visually**: a drag-and-drop flow designer for
  multi-step dialogues — messages, questions, delays, buttons — triggered by
  commands or keywords.
- **Reach your audience**: scheduled broadcasts with built-in A/B testing
  (try up to three variants on a slice of users, then roll out the winner),
  plus welcome messages, autoresponders, and drip campaigns.
- **An AI assistant** that can answer users on the bot's behalf, and one-click
  AI translation of your bot texts.
- **Ready-made Mini Apps**: seven templates including an online shop with
  Telegram Stars checkout, a loyalty hub, forms, and a prize wheel — the
  small web apps that open inside Telegram.
- **Practical extras**: a group file search that answers "does anyone have
  X?"-style questions automatically, a shared media library, and bot replies
  in 14 languages (dashboard in English, Turkish, German, and Russian).
- **Ops included**: clone a bot's whole setup to a new one, export/import
  configurations, automatic nightly backups, health monitoring with Telegram
  alerts when a bot goes down, and Prometheus metrics.

## Who is it for?

For community managers, small businesses, and makers who run several Telegram
bots and want one place to control them. It deploys as a single Docker
container (a one-command `docker compose up`), works well on cheap VPS or
home servers, and ships pre-configured for [Coolify](https://coolify.io).
Your data stays in your own SQLite database.
