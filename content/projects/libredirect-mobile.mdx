# LibRedirect Mobile

An Android app that reroutes links from large online services to
privacy-friendly frontends — before they ever reach your browser.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/sametcn99/libredirect-mobile) |
[Releases](https://github.com/sametcn99/libredirect-mobile/releases) |
[Upstream LibRedirect](https://github.com/libredirect)

## What is it?

You tap a YouTube link someone sent you. Normally that opens YouTube, with
everything that comes with it: ads, tracking, and your viewing habits tied to
an account. LibRedirect Mobile steps in at the moment the link opens and
rewrites it to a privacy-friendly alternative — for example an
[Invidious](https://redirect.invidious.io) instance for YouTube or a Redlib
instance for Reddit — and then hands the rewritten link to the browser you
choose.

It works the same way for many popular services, and it does all of this on
your device. No account, no backend server, no analytics. If a link can't be
routed, it simply opens normally — nothing breaks.

## What can it do?

- **Redirect popular services** like YouTube, Reddit, and X/Twitter to
  community-run, privacy-friendly frontends.
- **Strip tracking parameters** (`utm_*`, click IDs, and similar) from links
  before they open — even for links it doesn't redirect.
- **Unwrap redirect wrappers** like `google.com/url?q=…` and AMP pages
  offline, so you land on the real destination.
- **Open links your way**: in your favorite external browser, or in the
  built-in multi-tab browser that ships with the app.
- **The built-in browser** blocks known tracker and ad hosts, offers a clean
  reader mode, picture-in-picture video, bookmarks, and a private start page.
- **Fine-grained control**: enable or disable each service individually, pick
  which frontend and instance to use, add exceptions for certain sites or
  apps, and pause the whole thing for a while from a Quick Settings tile.
- **A "Why?" screen** that shows, for any link you paste, exactly which rule
  matched and what the app will do with it.

## Who is it for?

For anyone who wants big-platform links to open in lighter, ad-free,
tracker-free frontends without giving up their usual browser and apps. The
app's interface is available in English and Turkish, updates arrive as simple
APK downloads from GitHub Releases, and settings can be backed up to a single
file. It is released into the public domain (Unlicense).
