# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is technical readers who arrive to discover and read Samet Can Cincik's writing, engineering notes, and practical guides.

## Product Purpose

The site is Samet's personal publishing and work archive. Its primary job is to make the writing worth discovering and easy to read, while projects, gists, repositories, and the CV provide supporting evidence of hands-on software work.

Success means a visitor can quickly understand what Samet writes about, find a relevant piece, and continue exploring related work.

## Positioning

The site brings blog posts, practical technical references, software experiments, and project records into one searchable first-party archive rather than splitting them across a conventional portfolio and external publishing platforms.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse recent writing, search across posts, gists, projects, social links, and repositories, read long technical documents with code and diagrams, inspect projects, subscribe through RSS, or continue to GitHub and other profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing .NET 10 Blazor application and content services.
- Preserve the existing blog, gist, project, repository, CV, README, links, RSS, and redirect routes.
- Preserve site-wide search, but its placement and interaction may change.
- Continue rendering the existing MDX content, code blocks, tables, images, details, and Mermaid diagrams.
- Continue supporting Turkish and English content.
- The visual redesign remains dark-first.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Samet Can Cincik as the site identity.
- Keep the voice direct, technically specific, and personal rather than corporate.
- Preserve the existing favicon assets unless a later brand decision explicitly replaces them.

## Evidence on Hand

- Long-form posts in `content/posts/`.
- Practical technical references in `content/gists/`.
- Software project records in `content/projects/`.
- Career history and skills in `content/cv.mdx` and the downloadable CV files under `wwwroot/`.
- Existing GitHub repository integration, RSS feeds, social links, search API, and analytics events.
- No testimonials, customer claims, audience metrics, or commercial performance claims are available and they must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Writing leads; projects and credentials support it.
- Help readers discover a useful path instead of presenting an undifferentiated content inventory.
- Prefer strong editorial hierarchy and readable documents over repeated dashboard cards.
- Preserve fast access to the full archive through search and clear navigation.
- Let technical specificity and real artifacts establish credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive behavior across desktop and mobile web.
